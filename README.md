Strategic Product Positioning Analysis

📌 Overview

This project analyzes retail sales and product placement data to understand how positioning strategies impact sales performance and consumer behavior. Using a large retail dataset covering product sales records, shelf placement details, and category information, the project identifies key factors influencing revenue growth and translates them into actionable insights for retail and product strategy decisions.

Duration: Summer Internship Project, 2026

🎯 Problem Statement

Retail managers often need to make fast, informed decisions about product placement, shelf positioning, and category strategy — but sales and placement data can be complex and hard to interpret quickly. This project addresses that gap by analyzing how shelf zones, product categories, and visibility together impact overall revenue, then presenting those findings through interactive dashboards that make the data easy to act on.

🗂️ Dataset


Type: Retail sales and product placement dataset
Contents: Product sales records, shelf placement details, and product category information
Focus: Identifying which placement and positioning factors most strongly influence revenue


🛠️ Tools & Skills Used


Database: MySQL — for storing and managing retail data
Visualization: Tableau — for building interactive charts and dashboards
Web Application: Flask — for building the web application interface
Analysis: SQL querying, performance indicators, placement analysis metrics


🔍 Process


Data Storage & Management — organized retail sales and placement data using MySQL
Exploratory Analysis — examined how shelf zones, product categories, and visibility relate to sales performance
Dashboard Development — built 10 interactive charts and 2 detailed dashboards in Tableau to visualize product performance, shelf effectiveness, and category-wise sales trends
Web Interface — developed a Flask-based web application to make the analysis accessible and interactive
Insight Generation — translated data patterns into actionable recommendations for improving product positioning strategy


🎯 Purpose of the Project


Business Insight: Help retail managers quickly understand complex sales and placement data — specifically, how shelf zones, product categories, and visibility together impact overall revenue.
Revenue Optimization: By creating performance indicators and placement analysis metrics, the project doesn't just show past sales data — it provides actionable insights to improve product positioning strategies going forward.


📊 Key Insights

- *Placement effectiveness varies by category* — Clothing sells best at Front of Store (1,923.7), while Food does best at End-cap (1,813.6) and worst at Front of Store. One-size-fits-all placement doesn't work.

- *Electronics sales are placement-insensitive* — volume stays tight (1,726–1,760) across Aisle, End-cap, and Front of Store, suggesting these are planned purchases, not impulse-driven.

- *High foot-traffic zones drive stronger sales* — Aisle, End-cap, and Front of Store (High traffic) show larger sales volumes than Medium/Low-traffic zones.

- *Promoted items are priced higher, not lower* — ~₹29 avg. price when promoted vs. ~₹27–28 when not, across all categories — suggesting promotions here are tied to premium items, not discounts.

- *Pricing is fairly flat across categories* (₹27.6–28.6), meaning placement and foot traffic drive sales more than price does.
